Before adding individual vitamins

The current multivitamin already supplies vitamins A, C, D, E, K, the B-complex, iodine, zinc, selenium, and other ingredients. Do not add a single vitamin merely because it appears beneficial; first total the label amounts, diet, laboratory results, kidney function, and medication interactions.

  • Confirm the exact ALA, benfotiamine, magnesium, B6, manganese, iodine, and selenium totals with a pharmacist or clinician.
  • Ask whether vitamin D, B12, folate, iron studies/CBC, metabolic panel/eGFR, glucose/A1c, and thyroid tests are current.
  • Tim's ALA/benfotiamine regimen should remain labeled investigational and neurologist-supervised.
  • Do not change prescriptions or research-informed doses from this library.
